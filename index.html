SECTION 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1.

This Chargeback and Dispute Resolution Policy (the “Policy”) is issued by Premier PayFast (Private) Limited (“Premier PayFast”). This Policy establishes Premier PayFast’s comprehensive governance and operational framework for the management, processing and resolution of Chargebacks, the responsibilities of Merchants, including Sub-Merchants, Super Merchants and Aggregators, and prescribes the procedures, controls and escalation mechanisms that govern Chargeback-related activities across the Premier PayFast Platform.

1.2.

Without limitation, this Policy governs:

1.2.1.

the review and processing of Chargebacks from partner/Acquirer banks or Payment Schemes;

1.2.2.

communication with Merchants and Aggregators regarding Chargebacks and related disputes;

1.2.3.

the collection, review and submission of Fulfillment documentation and supporting evidence;

1.2.4.

dispute Representment, Pre-Arbitration and Arbitration proceedings;

1.2.5.

Settlement adjustment and withholding, reserve management and other risk mitigation measures implemented in connection with Chargeback exposure;

1.2.6.

recovery of Chargeback liabilities from Merchants and Super Merchants/Aggregators;

1.2.7.

Merchant risk classification and monitoring activities relating to Chargeback exposure; and

1.2.8.

Any ancillary activity required to ensure compliance with applicable Payment Scheme rules and regulatory requirements.

1.3.

This Policy applies to:

1.3.1.

All Merchants onboarded by Premier PayFast, whether directly or through an Aggregator/Super Merchant i.e. a Sub-Merchant; and

1.3.2.

All Aggregators and Super Merchants operating through the PayFast Platform;

in respect of all Transactions processed through the Premier PayFast Platform.

1.4.

This Policy forms part of, and is incorporated by reference into, the Agreement, and is the Chargeback policy and the dispute resolution standard operating procedure referred to in the Agreement. This Policy shall be read and applied together with the Agreement and the Applicable Law. The provisions of this Policy shall apply to matters addressed herein, including where the Agreement is silent on such matters. Where any provision of this Policy is inconsistent with an express provision of the Agreement and/or the Applicable Law, the relevant provision of the Agreement or Applicable Law.

1.5.

Nothing in this Policy limits, waives or qualifies any right, entitlement or remedy available to Premier PayFast under the Agreement, the Payment Scheme Rules or Applicable Law, whether or not that right is referred to in this Policy.

1.6.

Premier PayFast may amend this Policy to reflect any change in the Payment Scheme Rules, Applicable Law or a Regulatory Instruction, and shall make the amended Policy available periodically.

SECTION 4. CHARGEBACK PROCESS

Every Chargeback, whether relating to a Direct Merchant or a Sub-Merchant, is handled through the stages set out below, from initiation through to resolution. Each stage sets out the applicable process, responsibilities and, where relevant, the corresponding impact on settlement. Where a Chargeback relates to a Sub-Merchant, the applicable responsibilities of the Super Merchant or Aggregator are also identified at the relevant stage.

4.1. Stage 1: Dispute Initiation by the Cardholder

4.1.1.

A Chargeback arises in the following sequence:

(a)

the Cardholder raises a dispute with the Issuer in respect of a Transaction;

(b)

the Issuer reviews the dispute against the applicable Payment Scheme Rules and the relevant Chargeback Reason;

(c)

where the dispute meets the given criteria, the Issuer raises a Chargeback and notifies the Acquirer; and

(d)

the Acquirer notifies Premier PayFast of the Chargeback, the Chargeback Reason, the disputed amount and the applicable response deadline.

4.1.2.

Stages (a) to (c) occur before Premier PayFast is involved.

4.1.3.

An Issuer may issue a Retrieval Request in respect of a Transaction before raising a Chargeback. Where Premier PayFast receives a Retrieval Request it shall notify the Merchant, and the Merchant shall provide the documentation requested within the period stated in that notice. A Retrieval Request does not of itself give rise to a Settlement Withholding.

4.2. Stage 2: Chargeback Notification

4.2.1.

Premier PayFast notifies the Merchant of the Chargeback as soon as reasonably practicable upon receipt of a Chargeback Notification from the Acquirer (and, where a Sub-Merchant is involved, also the Super Merchant or Aggregator), including the reason, Transaction reference, amount, response deadline, and required Fulfillment Documentation.

4.2.2.

Premier PayFast shall simultaneously withhold the relevant disputed amount from the Settlement otherwise payable to the Merchant, Super Merchant or Aggregator for twenty-five (25) days from the relevant Settlement date, or for such longer period as may be reasonably necessary where the Chargeback remains unresolved, the supporting documentation is incomplete, or the Acquiring Bank or applicable Payment Scheme requires further information or documentation. Any such withholding shall apply only on amounts otherwise payable by Premier PayFast to the relevant Merchant, Super Merchant or Aggregator; it is not, and does not constitute, the holding of consumer or participant funds within the meaning of the Payment Systems and Electronic Funds Transfer Act, 2007.

4.3. Stage 3: Merchant Response and Fulfillment

4.3.1.

Upon receipt of the Chargeback Notification, the Merchant must review it carefully, noting the Reason, disputed amount, and the response deadline. Thereafter, the Merchant shall have five (5) Business Days from the date of Premier PayFast's notification to submit all relevant Fulfillment Documentation to Premier PayFast for review and submission.

4.3.2.

Where the applicable Payment Scheme or Acquirer deadline requires a shorter period, Premier PayFast shall notify the Merchant of the shorter deadline, and that deadline shall apply. Where Premier PayFast requests further or clarifying documents during its review, the Merchant shall provide them within the timeline mentioned by Premier PayFast.

4.3.3.

The Merchant must elect within this fulfillment period to either: (a) accept the Chargeback liability (in which case the Settlement Withholding shall be applied in satisfaction of the Chargeback amount), or (b) contest the Chargeback by submitting supporting documentation and information, including relevant fulfilment records, as may be required under the applicable Payment Scheme Rules or Acquirer requirements to substantiate the Merchant's position to the satisfaction of the Acquirer and in accordance with the applicable Payment Scheme Rules.

4.3.4.

Where a Sub-Merchant is involved, the Super Merchant or Aggregator shall be responsible for ensuring that the Sub-Merchant is responsive and provides the required Fulfillment Documentation within the given time frame and shall remain liable for the Chargeback and any related amounts arising in respect of the Sub-Merchant.

4.3.5.

Premier PayFast may request any of the documentation listed below in Section 5, from the date of the Chargeback Notification, and may request further documentation at any stage of the dispute, including at Pre-Arbitration and Arbitration.

4.3.6.

Where the Merchant fails to provide the required documentation within any extended timeframe, or the submitted evidence remains incomplete, insufficient, or does not satisfy the applicable Payment Scheme requirements, Reason Code criteria, or Acquirer requirements, PayFast may be unable to effectively represent the Chargeback. In such cases, the Chargeback may be deemed accepted and processed in accordance with the applicable Payment Scheme rules and Acquirer procedures. Any resulting Chargeback liability, fees, penalties or other amounts shall be borne by the relevant Merchant or, where applicable, the Super Merchant or Aggregator in respect of the relevant Sub-Merchant, and shall be recovered in accordance with the Agreement and this Policy.

4.4. Stage 4: Review and Submission to the Acquirer

4.4.1.

Premier PayFast shall review all the Fulfillment Documentation for completeness, relevance and adequacy to the Chargeback Reason prior to submission to the Acquirer. Where the documentation is incomplete or does not adequately address the applicable Chargeback Reason or Acquirer requirements, Premier Payfast shall be return it to the Merchant for correction or supplementation, which the Merchant must provide promptly to to meet the applicable Payment Scheme timeframe for submission to the Acquirer. Once the documentation is considered satisfactory, Premier PayFast shall submit it to the Acquirer within the prescribed turnaround time for Representment to the Issuer.

4.4.2.

Premier PayFast shall use reasonable effort to submit the Representment within the applicable window, and is not responsible for a Representment that cannot be submitted, or is rejected, because the Chargeback was time-barred due to delay in the provision of Fulfillment Documentation, incomplete Fulfillment Documentation or where the Fulfillment Documentation does not meet or satisfy the criteria of the relevant Payment Scheme.

4.4.3.

The Submission of a Representment package does not guarantee a favorable outcome. The final determination remains subject to the evaluation of the Issuer, Acquirer, and applicable Payment Scheme. Incomplete, insufficient, or non-compliant evidence may result in the Representment being rejected and the Chargeback being upheld.

4.5. Stage 5: Issuer Review and Decision

4.5.1.

The Issuer reviews the Representment. Where the Fulfilment Documentation are complete, sufficient and satisfy the criteria of the relevant Payment Scheme, the Chargeback is closed in the Merchant's favour and any deferred amount is released in the next Settlement cycle. Where a Chargeback is upheld following Issuer review and the Issuer Initiates Pre-Arbitration proceedings, the Acquirer communicates the decision to Premier PayFast.

4.6. Stage 6: Pre-Arbitration

4.6.1.

Where the Chargeback is upheld and the Issuer escalates the matter, it proceeds to Pre-Arbitration.

4.6.2.

Premier PayFast shall assess each Pre-Arbitration case before determining whether to recommend proceeding with the matter.

4.6.3.

The Merchant (and, where applicable, the Super Merchant or Aggregator) will be informed of the assessment of the case and the associated costs and risks before any decision is made on whether to continue.

4.6.4.

Where the Merchant elects not to proceed to Pre-Arbitration having been informed of the associated costs and risks, Premier PayFast will request for closure of the Pre-Arbitration proceedings and the Chargeback amount is adjusted through the withheld amount or recovered in accordance with Section 8

4.6.5.

Where the Merchant, having been informed of the Chargeback team's assessment, the potential costs and risks associated with proceeding, nevertheless elects to contest the Pre-Arbitration, and requests that the matter be submitted to the Acquirer and/or Payment Scheme, Premier PayFast will proceed with the submission, subject to the applicable Payment Scheme rules and internal approval requirements. Settlement Withholding may be applied over the next settlement payable to cover the Pre-Arbitration costs, any related fee, and an Arbitration fee hold may also apply.

4.6.6.

Where the Pre-Arbitration is proceeded with and is subsequently determined adversely against the Acquirer/Merchant, the Merchant shall remain liable for the Chargeback amount and any applicable Arbitration fees, scheme-related fees, penalties or other costs arising in connection with the matter, in accordance with this Policy and the applicable Payment Scheme rules. Premier PayFast, in its capacity as a payment service provider and intermediary, shall bear no financial liability in respect of Pre-Arbitration costs or adverse determinations.

4.7. Stage 7: Arbitration

4.7.1.

Where Pre-Arbitration is exhausted and the Issuer declines to accept the Acquirer's response, the matter escalates to formal Arbitration before the applicable Payment Scheme, whose determination is final and binding. Compelling evidence, fully aligned with applicable Payment Scheme guidelines, is essential at this stage.

4.7.2.

If the outcome is not in favour of the Merchant, all Arbitration fees, related charges, and the principal Chargeback amount become recoverable from the Merchant (and, where recovery from a Sub-Merchant is insufficient, from the Super Merchant or Aggregator). Premier PayFast, acting as a payment service provider and intermediary, bears no financial liability for Arbitration costs or adverse determinations.

4.7.3.

A Chargeback resolved in the Merchant's favour results in release of the Settlement Withholding and in the next applicable settlement cycle

4.8. Stage 8: Resolution and Recovery

4.8.1.

Where the Chargeback is accepted or upheld, the liability is recovered in accordance with Section 8 and, where the Merchant is a Sub-Merchant, Section 11.

4.9.

Where, at any stage of the Chargeback process, the Chargeback cannot be submitted, Represented or otherwise progressed within the applicable Payment Scheme or Acquirer timeframe due to the Merchant's delay, failure to respond, or failure to provide complete, sufficient or compliant documentation or information, and the Chargeback is subsequently upheld or otherwise results in a liability against Premier PayFast, the resulting Chargeback amount, fees, penalties and other applicable costs or amounts shall be borne by the Merchant or, where applicable, the Super Merchant or Aggregator in respect of the relevant Sub-Merchant.

SECTION 5. FULFILMENT DOCUMENTATION

5.1.

The Merchant shall maintain, and shall provide to Premier PayFast on request, all records necessary to contest the Chargeback, establish the legitimacy of a disputed Transaction and the delivery or performance of the goods or services to which it relates. Premier PayFast may request any of the documentation described in this Section 5 from the date of the Chargeback Notification, and may request further documentation at any stage of the dispute, including at Pre-Arbitration and at Arbitration.

5.2.

Fulfilment requirements may vary depending on the applicable Payment Scheme, dispute category, Chargeback Reason / Chargeback Reason Code, dispute stage, regulatory requirements, and Acquirer instructions. Merchants are responsible for maintaining and providing all records necessary to support the legitimacy and Fulfillment of the disputed Transaction within the applicable Payment Scheme timelines. For a Sub-Merchant, the Super Merchant or Aggregator is responsible for ensuring that the Merchant maintains and provides the necessary support and documentation within the applicable timeframe.

5.3.

Fulfillment Documentation may include, without limitation:

5.3.1.

proof of delivery, including signed proof of delivery, courier tracking records, delivery confirmation or shipment documentation;

5.3.2.

Cardholder authorization, signed order forms or Transaction confirmation records;

5.3.3.

the invoice, receipt, order details or Transaction records;

5.3.4.

the refund, cancellation, shipping, return or exchange policy applicable at the time of the Transaction, together with evidence that it was communicated to the Cardholder;

5.3.5.

correspondence with the Cardholder, including email, chat records, SMS, call logs or other communication records;

5.3.6.

access logs, IP logs, device information, authentication records or usage logs, where the Transaction relates to digital goods or services;

5.3.7.

evidence of Cardholder authentication, including 3D Secure authentication records;

5.3.8.

any documentation establishing that the goods or services were delivered, received, accessed, used or otherwise fulfilled in accordance with the Merchant's terms; and

5.3.9.

any further documentation, record or evidence required by the Payment Scheme, the Chargeback Reason, the Acquirer, a Regulatory Instruction or a subsequent stage of the dispute.

SECTION 6. CHARGEBACK RISK CLASSIFICATION

6.1.

Every Merchant, whether onboarded directly or as a Sub-Merchant, is classified into Category A (Low Risk) or Category B (High Risk) at onboarding, and monitored and reviewed periodically thereafter. Classification determines Settlement treatment and informs the Risk-Based Settlement Hold Mechanism described in Clause 7.6.

6.2. Category A

6.2.1.

A Merchant may be classified as Category A where, having regard to its overall risk profile and the assessment undertaken by the Business Team, Risk Management Team and/or senior management, all of the following factors are present, as applicable:

(a)

an established, large scale business of sound financial standing;

(b)

a verifiable operating history and a satisfactory risk profile

(c)

a normal or standard risk merchant category code;

(d)

Transactions are authenticated using 3D Secure;

(e)

a Chargeback-to-Sales Ratio at or below 2%;

(f)

Fulfillment Documentation submitted is within the period specified in Clause 4.3;

(g)

no adverse fraud, refund or compliance trend;

6.2.2.

Standard Settlement processing applies to a Category A Merchants, and no Settlement Withholding is generally applied in advance of resolution, save where Clause 7.2.1(b) or Clause 7.2.1(c) applies.

6.3. Category B

6.3.1.

A Merchant may be classified in Category B where any one or more of the following factors is present:

(a)

limited financial capacity or evidence of financial weakness;

(b)

a merchant category code designated as high risk under the Payment Scheme Rules, Acquirer or by Premier PayFast;

(c)

a predominantly international Cardholder base;

(d)

card-not-present Transactions processed without 3D Secure authentication;

(e)

the Merchant deals in goods or services characterized by structurally elevated dispute rates;

(f)

a Chargeback-to-Sales Ratio above 2% in any calendar month;

(g)

repeated failure to meet the response periods set out in this Policy;

(h)

an adverse fraud, refund or compliance finding;

(i)

no adverse recovery trend;

(j)

irregular, inconsistent or minimal Transaction activity;

(k)

any other factor identified by the business team, risk management team and/or senior management of Premier Payfast as indicating an elevated risk profile.

6.3.2.

A Settlement Withholding is applied in respect of each Chargeback raised against a Category B Merchant, in accordance with Clause 7.2, and a Security Reserve may be required under the Agreement.

6.4.

The classification of a Merchant under this Policy shall be subject to ongoing assessment and monitoring by the Business Team, Risk Management Team and/or senior management. The relevant team(s) may consider the Merchant's onboarding information, transaction activity, Chargeback and refund history, fraud and compliance indicators, business sector, operating history, financial standing and any other relevant risk factors in determining whether the Merchant continues to meet the applicable Category criteria.

6.5.

Premier PayFast monitors the profile of the Merchants on a monthly basis. A Merchant classified in Category A may be reclassified to Category B where any of the Category B criteria is met. Reclassification to Category B shall result in the Merchant becoming subject to the additional requirements and controls applicable to Category B, including, where determined by Premier PayFast, the requirement to maintain a Security Reserve.

SECTION 7. SETTLEMENT WITHHOLDING AND ADJUSTMENT MECHANISM

7.1.

Upon receipt of a Chargeback Notification or otherwise from the Acquirer, Premier PayFast shall, in accordance with the relevant Agreement, the applicable Payment Scheme Rules, the Acquirer's instructions and applicable law or regulatory requirements, implement a Settlement Adjustment or Settlement Withholding whereby it will either withhold, defer or adjust the Merchant's pending Settlement payable to the extent necessary to satisfy the relevant Chargeback liability or until resolution.

7.2. Settlement Withholding

7.2.1.

On receiving notice of a Chargeback from the Acquirer, Premier PayFast shall withhold the disputed amount from Settlement where:

(a)

the Merchant is a Category B Merchant;

(b)

the Acquirer, the Payment Scheme or a Regulatory Instruction requires the amount to be withheld; or

(c)

a risk indicator described in Clause 7.6.2 is present in respect of the Merchant or, where the Merchant is a Sub-Merchant, in respect of the Super Merchant or Aggregator.

7.2.2.

Settlement Withholding is limited to the disputed amount, together with any Payment Scheme fee, Pre-Arbitration amount or Arbitration fee arising on the same Chargeback, and applies only to amounts otherwise payable by Premier PayFast to the Merchant, the Super Merchant or Aggregator.

7.2.3.

Where none of the circumstances in Clause 7.2.1 applies, Settlement may continue in the ordinary course and any liability arising on resolution of the Chargeback is recovered under Section 8.

7.3.

Where Clause 7.2 applies, a Settlement Withholding is applied, or an existing withholding is extended, on:

7.3.1.

receipt of notice of a Chargeback from the Acquirer, specifying the Chargeback Reason, the disputed amount, the currency, the Transaction reference and the response deadline;

7.3.2.

a request by the Acquirer, the Payment Scheme or a competent authority for further information or documentation, which extends the period of the withholding;

7.3.3.

escalation of the dispute to Pre-Arbitration, requiring a further amount to be withheld to cover the Pre-Arbitration amount and any related fee;

7.3.4.

escalation of the dispute to Arbitration, requiring a further amount to be withheld to cover the Arbitration fees and the principal Chargeback amount; or

7.3.5.

receipt of a further Chargeback where the Settlement then payable is insufficient to cover the aggregate disputed amounts, requiring an incremental withholding.

7.4. Duration

7.4.1.

A Settlement Withholding may usually remain in effect for twenty-five (25) calendar days from the date on which it is applied, or from such later date as the Acquirer or the Payment Scheme requires the disputed amount to be held pending resolution, or for such other period as the Payment Scheme Rules or the Acquirer require.

7.4.2.

The withholding period may be extended for so long as is necessary where the Acquirer, the Payment Scheme, or a competent authority requests further information or documentation, or where the dispute proceeds to Pre-Arbitration, Arbitration or any other Payment Scheme process, and in each case until the dispute is concluded.

7.4.3.

Where a withholding is required pursuant to a Regulatory Instruction, it continues for such period as may be required, or until the matter is resolved to the satisfaction of or otherwise concluded by, the relevant authority.

7.4.4.

Where an additional Chargeback is received and the Merchant's pending Settlement is insufficient, the Settlement amount payable to the Merchant shall be kept withheld to reflect the additional Chargeback amount.

7.5. Release and Settlement Adjustment

7.5.1.

Where a Chargeback is resolved in the Merchant's favour, Premier PayFast shall release the Settlement Withholding applied in respect of it in the next available Settlement cycle.

7.5.2.

Where a Chargeback is upheld, Premier PayFast shall apply the Settlement Withholding in satisfaction of the resulting liability by way of Settlement Adjustment, and shall recover any shortfall in accordance with Section 8.

7.5.3.

The amount recovered comprises the disputed Transaction amount together with all Payment Scheme fees, Pre-Arbitration and Arbitration fees, assessments, fines, penalties and costs arising in connection with the Chargeback, and the MDR, which is earned on processing and is not refunded where a Chargeback is upheld.

7.6. Risk-Based Settlement Hold

7.6.1.

Separately from a Settlement Withholding in respect of a specific Chargeback, and whether or not a Chargeback is then pending, Premier PayFast may, acting reasonably, in good faith and on objectively identifiable basis, pause, extend or reapply a hold on Settlement otherwise payable to a Merchant, Super Merchant or an Aggregator, where reasonably necessary to manage or mitigate an identified financial, fraud, compliance or other material risk. Any such hold shall be proportionate to the nature and level of the relevant risk and may remain in place for such period as Premier PayFast reasonably considers necessary to address that risk.

7.6.2.

A Risk-Based Settlement Hold may be triggered by:

(a)

an elevated risk profile based on transaction and Chargeback history;

(b)

a material adverse change in the Chargeback or refund trend, or in the Chargeback-to-Sales Ratio;

(c)

adverse fraud or compliance indicators, including sanctions screening alert, a politically exposed person association or adverse media finding;

(d)

a sustained pattern of non-response to Chargeback notifications;

(e)

cessation or material reduction in Transaction activity;

(f)

a Regulatory Instruction; or

(g)

any other indicator of increased financial risk to Premier PayFast.

7.6.3.

For a Super Merchant or Aggregator, this assessment may also take into account the risk profile and activity of its Sub-Merchant portfolio as a whole, and the hold may accordingly be applied at the individual Sub-Merchant level or at the Super Merchant or Aggregator level.

7.6.4.

Premier PayFast shall notify the Merchant, or the Super Merchant/Aggregator where applicable, if a Risk-Based Settlement Hold is applied, stating the reason and, where reasonably practicable, the circumstances in which it may be released.

7.6.5.

Premier PayFast shall review the continuing need for a Risk-Based Settlement Hold at least once every thirty (30) days. A hold applied pursuant to a Regulatory Instruction continues until the relevant clearance, release or final determination is received.

7.6.6.

Where the Merchant is a Sub-Merchant, the assessment under this Clause may take into account the risk profile and activity of the Super Merchant/Aggregator's Sub-Merchant portfolio as a whole, and the hold may be applied at either the level of the individual Sub-Merchant or of the Super Merchant/Aggregator.

7.6.7.

A Risk-Based Settlement Hold is in addition to, and not in substitution for, any Security Reserve and any Settlement Withholding.
